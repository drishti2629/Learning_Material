A simplified cognitive story — not neuroscience. Every loss, gradient and update below is real, computed on the lab's seeded decoder (seed 701) with real backprop and a real SGD step.

As YOU read a sentence, you predict what comes next — then the world (or a teacher) shows you the real answer, and you feel surprised in proportion to how wrong you were. That surprise is exactly what trains a brain, and it is exactly what trains this decoder. Pick a real training record above, or just watch.
